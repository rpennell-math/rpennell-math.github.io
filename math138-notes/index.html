How to use these notes

Click any section below to jump straight to it in the PDF. Inside the notes, colored boxes mark definitions, theorems, lemmas, corollaries, properties and formulas, and methods (the key is on the first page). Proofs appear in gray side-bar blocks and remarks in gold. Results that need tools beyond the course are marked “stated without proof.”
