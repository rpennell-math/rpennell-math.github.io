Calculus

Limits

The ε–δ Definition of a Limit

Play the limit game: your opponent picks ε, you answer with δ. Drag on the graph to watch where the function stays in the band and where it escapes, for limits that work (x², sin x/x, x sin(1/x)) and ones that fail (a jump, sin(1/x)), or type in your own function.

 Limits

The Squeeze Theorem, ε–δ Style

The proof as a picture: find δ for the lower bound, δ for the upper bound, take the smaller one, and the trapped function has nowhere to go. Examples include x² sin(1/x) and sin x/x, plus one where the bounds disagree and the theorem says nothing.

Precalculus & Trigonometry

Conic Sections

Which Way Does the Ellipse Stretch?

The bigger denominator wins. Drag the denominators under x² and y² and watch the ellipse stretch horizontally, vertically, or become a circle, with the half-axes, a, b, c, and the foci marked.

 Law of Sines

The Ambiguous Case (SSA): Why 0, 1, or 2 Triangles?

Given angle A, side a, and side b, swing side a around vertex C and count where it lands. The Law of Sines work updates as you drag, showing when sin B is too big, when there's exactly one right triangle, and when both B and 180° − B fit.
